Connecting VLA navigation decisions with vehicle dynamics so autonomous driving plans are dynamically feasible, not merely semantically plausible.对齐 VLA 高层导航与车辆动力学，使自动驾驶规划不仅符合语义指令，也满足车辆的动力学可行性约束。

01 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Research motivation研究动机

A navigation model may produce decisions that are reasonable at the language or route level but difficult for a physical vehicle to execute. DynAlign focuses on this gap between high-level intelligence and motion feasibility.高层导航模型生成的决策可能在语义或路线层面合理，却难以被真实车辆执行。DynAlign 聚焦高层智能与车辆运动可行性之间的差距。

02 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Research focus研究重点

The manuscript studies alignment between vision-language-action navigation and vehicle dynamics, with the objective of making autonomous-driving behavior dynamically feasible. Implementation specifics and quantitative claims are intentionally omitted here until public release.论文研究视觉语言动作导航与车辆动力学的对齐，目标是提高自动驾驶行为的动力学可行性。具体实现和定量实验在公开前不在主页披露。
