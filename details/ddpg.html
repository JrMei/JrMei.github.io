01 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Problem setup任务定义

A driving agent must choose safe lane-changing behavior in response to neighboring vehicles. The project provides a sandbox for comparing reinforcement-learning decision strategies.智能体根据邻车状态执行安全换道决策，项目提供用于比较不同强化学习决策策略的仿真实验环境。

Relationship to later work与后续研究的关联

The project established early experience in policy learning and simulation, informing subsequent work on structured-intent planning and learning-based vehicle control.该项目积累了策略学习和仿真实验经验，为后续结构化意图规划与学习式车辆控制研究提供了基础。
