How can off-road VLA prioritize decision-relevant terrain and carry experience forward through continuous vehicle–environment interaction?越野 VLA 如何主动选择与当前决策相关的地形信息，并利用连续车辆—场景交互形成有用的时序上下文？

01 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Active terrain perception主动地形感知

Trajectory-guided gaze connects candidate driving trajectories to relevant visual and terrain cues. The goal is to direct model capacity toward decision-critical information rather than treating every observed region equally.通过候选轨迹引导主动注视，将规划意图关联至关键视觉与地形线索，使模型优先处理决策相关信息，而非等权关注所有区域。

02 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Action-conditioned temporal context动作条件时序记忆

A temporal-memory branch is designed to connect recent observation, motion context and action outcomes, supporting decisions when single-frame perception is insufficient.构建动作条件时序记忆分支，联合历史观察、车辆运动上下文与动作结果，为单帧感知不足的场景提供连续决策依据。

03 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Reproducibility boundary复现边界

The Wild-Drive single-trajectory baseline was audited in the project. Public baseline implementations and paper-level experimental configurations must be distinguished; this portfolio does not present an unverified end-to-end closed-loop score.项目已开展 Wild-Drive 单轨迹基线审计。开源基线与论文级实验配置需严格区分；主页不展示未经确认的完整闭环成绩。
