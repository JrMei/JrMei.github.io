01 / OVERVIEW内容概览

The question研究问题

On-ramp merging requires a vehicle to balance safety, efficiency and comfort while responding to surrounding traffic. Directly mapping a learned decision to low-level action can produce unstable intent switches.匝道汇入需要在动态交通交互中兼顾安全、效率与舒适性。若将学习策略输出直接用于底层动作，驾驶意图容易发生频繁切换。

02 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Method overview核心方法

A PPO policy predicts discrete driving intent; an SI-Filter smooths transitions; a merge coordinator handles interactions; and data-driven MPC plans executable motion. These components have complementary roles rather than replacing physical constraints with a black box.使用 PPO 预测离散驾驶意图，经 SI-Filter 平滑、Merge Coordinator 协调汇入交互，再由数据驱动 MPC 输出运动规划。模块分工明确，使学习决策与可执行控制形成闭环。

03 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Evaluation context评测范围

The framework has been evaluated in simulated mixed-traffic on-ramp scenarios. Results are experiment-specific and are not presented as real-world driving guarantees.已在混合交通匝道汇入仿真场景中开展评测。所有仿真结果均受实验设置约束，不等同于实车安全保证。
