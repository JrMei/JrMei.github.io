01 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Architectural concept分层架构

A high-level driving model interprets scene context and provides planning targets; a lower-level controller handles executable vehicle motion and wheel-level actions. The interface between the two layers is central to this exploration.上层驾驶模型负责理解场景并输出规划目标；底层控制器负责可执行的车辆运动与轮端控制，两层之间的状态与动作接口是研究重点。

02 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Vehicle platform底盘平台

Four-wheel independent drive and steering provide additional control authority but also complicate action representation, dynamics modeling and safety constraints.四轮独立驱动与转向提升控制自由度，也使动作空间、动力学建模和安全约束更加复杂。

03 / OVERVIEW内容概览

Platform plan实验平台

Isaac Lab and related simulation tooling are candidate platforms for policy learning and controlled evaluation. Hardware deployment must be presented as a future or independently verified stage.Isaac Lab 等仿真平台被列为策略学习与受控评测候选；实车部署应作为未来阶段或在独立验证后更新。
