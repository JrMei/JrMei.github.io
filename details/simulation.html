CARLA / Bench2DriveCARLA / Bench2Drive

A platform for autonomous-driving model experiments, route-oriented evaluation and VLA-related supervised fine-tuning workflows. Training and test variants are tracked explicitly.面向自动驾驶模型实验、基于路线的评估以及 VLA 监督微调流程，明确区分训练集、验证集及各测试配置。

02 / OVERVIEW内容概览

SUMO / TraCISUMO / TraCI

Traffic-interaction simulation supports intent learning, highway merging experiments and reproducible comparison of driving decisions.交通交互仿真用于意图学习、匝道汇入研究以及驾驶决策方法的可复现对比。

03 / OVERVIEW内容概览

BeamNG.techBeamNG.tech

Vehicle-dynamics-oriented simulation enables control-interface checks and safety-critical scenarios such as stopping in front of static obstacles. A working simulation interface should not be confused with a validated end-to-end controller.车辆动力学仿真适合验证控制接口与前方静态障碍刹停等安全场景；可运行的仿真接口并不等同于已验证的端到端控制系统。
