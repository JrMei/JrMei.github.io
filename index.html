I'm Jiarui Mei (梅家锐), a graduate student at Tongji University working on autonomous driving and embodied intelligence.

My research brings together vision-language-action models, reinforcement learning, and model predictive control. I care about turning promising algorithms into systems that can be tested in simulation and, ultimately, on real vehicles.
